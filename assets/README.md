# Profile artwork

## Noematrix

- Official source: https://www.noematrix.ai/image/common/logo.png
- Original artwork: 609 × 144 PNG, retrieved from the corporate homepage on 2026-10-15.
- Profile assets preserve every original pixel and add 24px of clear space on all sides. The dark-theme version has a white background for contrast. Neither version is AI-upscaled or claimed to be a vector.

## Tsinghua University

- Official specification: https://vi.tsinghua.edu.cn/gk/xxbz/bzzh1/xhyhszywbzzzyzh.htm
- Vector source: https://vi.tsinghua.edu.cn/download/VisualIdentity-cn.pdf
- Artwork: A-3-10, PDF page 30, the horizontal emblem and bilingual university-name signature.
- Extracted the five complete original vector paths with their fills, proportions, spacing and transforms preserved. The SVG viewport includes clear space of twice one-fifteenth of the emblem diameter on each side.
- The dark-theme asset adds a white backing without recolouring or clipping the official artwork.
- Both experience logos display at 200px wide in the profile README and link to their institutions.

## OpenYoink

- Source: `https://github.com/MuQY1818/OpenYoink/blob/main/OpenYoink/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon-256.png`.
- The original 256 × 256 PNG is reused unchanged as `openyoink-icon.png`, preserving transparency; it displays at 64px wide in the Open Source section.

## macbot

- Source: `https://github.com/MuQY1818/macbot/blob/main/assets/logo-mark.svg` (the project's own pixel-art mark, rectangles only).
- Reused unchanged as `macbot-mark.svg`; it displays at 64px wide in the Open Source section.

## China University of Mining and Technology

- Same asset as the homepage: the official monochrome wordmark from `https://www.cumt.edu.cn/images/logo.png`, recoloured to CUMT blue `#00549f` with alpha kept. It displays at 150px wide in the Experience table.

## Fudan University

- Same asset as the homepage Experience card: the official white monochrome logo from `https://www.fudan.edu.cn/_upload/site/00/02/2/logo.png`, recoloured to Fudan blue `#0E419C` (near-white pixels only, so the source drop shadow does not turn into a halo) and trimmed to 221 × 64. It displays at 150px wide in the Experience table; `teai-logo.svg` is kept in the repo but no longer used there.
